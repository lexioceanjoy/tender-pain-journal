# ADR 0002: Use Tender as the working product name

## Decision

Use **Tender** as the working product name and in the project documentation.

## Why

The name is warm, approachable, and connected to the experience of pain without presenting the product as a clinical diagnosis or treatment service.

## Important limit

This is not a trademark clearance. Before public launch, perform an appropriate trademark, domain, and social-handle availability review for the intended markets.
